# afdEP v2.1: Masked DeepEP adapted to AFD

Baseline: **Masked DeepEP** = the vendored DeepEP V2 kernels with `role_aware=False`
(union world, dummy Attention expert slots, upstream combine). v2.1 changes are
opt-in on the role path (`role_aware=True`, one NVLink domain); generic
instantiations keep the upstream template arguments and kernels.

## 1. FFN combine reduction

Upstream compiles the FFN reduction with a branch-free top-k prefix of
`ceil(top_k / ranks)`, where `ranks` counts the dummy Attention ranks, and unrolls
by four. In 2A2F that prefix is 2 of the ~4 slots each FFN actually reduces, and
unroll 4 spills (120–160-byte stack frames at the 64–72-register transport
budget). Role-path FFNs now use `ceil(top_k / ffn_ranks)` and unroll 2 (64-byte
frames, no extra spill). Attention ranks reduce nothing; they receive the same
arguments so every rank instantiates the same collective protocol.

Communication-only, 4 × H100, 2A2F, E128/top-k 8, capacity 2,048, BF16, graphs,
24 transport SMs. Median µs (Masked) and geometric-mean paired change of three
fresh-process repeats; parentheses count repeats faster than Masked.

+1.7% to −0.8% against Masked in the same screens. Other screened settings were

expected 4 was 2–10% slower at H4096. They are retained in the record.
